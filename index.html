12 — Questions

Good to know.

What is appointment setting?
Appointment setting means someone else finds your leads, contacts them, qualifies them and books them into your calendar as sales calls. FlowBuild AI does it end to end for Irish businesses: we run the Meta ads, reply to every enquiry instantly, call each lead and book the ones who are ready to buy.
How is this different from a lead generation company?
Most lead generation companies hand you names and phone numbers and leave the chasing to you. We go further: every lead is answered in seconds, called by our team, qualified and booked in. You get sales calls in your calendar, not a spreadsheet of leads.
What types of businesses do you work with?
Businesses across Ireland selling contracts worth €5,000 or more: home improvement companies and contractors, solar and energy installers, clinics and professional services. If the numbers don't work for your business, we'll tell you on the first call.
What counts as a qualified sales appointment?
A customer in your area, with the budget and timeframe you set, who is genuinely interested and has booked a sales call or visit with your team.
How does the guarantee work?
Before you start, we research your market and forecast your best, average and lowest case. Your lowest forecast becomes your guaranteed minimum number of sales appointments, in writing. If we haven't hit it within 90 days of going live, we keep working, with no extra management fee, until we do. If even your lowest forecast wouldn't make you money, we won't take you on.
Do you guarantee sales?
No. We guarantee sales appointments. Closing the job is down to you and your team.
Do I pay if they don't show up?
No. You only pay for qualified appointments that actually take place.
What does it cost?
It depends on your market and goals, so we go through it on the call once we've looked at your numbers. There's no retainer, and you only pay for appointments that actually happen. Your ad spend is paid straight to Meta.
I've never run ads before. Is that a problem?
Not at all. You don't need any existing ad spend. We set up and manage everything.
Who owns what you build?
You do. Your ad account, your CRM, your automations and your leads. If we ever part ways, you keep all of it.
Do you still build websites?
Yes. We design and build custom websites for Irish businesses. See our web design page for our work.
