The engine

Six moving parts.
You keep all of them.

Nothing here is a black box. If you wanted to run it yourself after we finish, you could.

01
Signals

Work out what a buyer looks like before they buy

We start with the customers you already won and look at what was happening in those businesses at the time. A funding round. A new head of department. A change in the software they run. Those patterns become the thing we watch for across the rest of your market, and we keep watching, because the list of companies showing them changes every week.

02
The list

Built to that profile, not bought off a shelf

Bought lists are why most cold email fails. We build yours against the profile instead, score each account on how closely it actually matches, and identify the person who owns the decision rather than whoever the database had on file. Every contact is verified before it goes anywhere near a campaign. Your do not contact list is loaded on day one and respected from then on.

03
Infrastructure

Your real domain never sends a thing

This is the part people get wrong and only notice a year later, when their invoices stop reaching clients. We register separate lookalike domains, point them at your real site, warm the inboxes properly before anything goes out, and hold daily volume per inbox deliberately low. Inbox health gets checked continuously. Anything starting to drift comes out before it costs you deliverability.

04
Campaigns

Written off something real, approved by you

A merge field with someone's company name in it is not personalisation and buyers stopped falling for it years ago. Each message references the actual signal that put the account on the list. You read the sequences and scripts and sign them off before a single send. From there we test angles against real reply data and cut whatever is not earning replies, including things we were sure would work.

05
Qualification

An SDR Agent on your account, filtering before you see it

Interest is not the same as a fit, and your team should not be the ones discovering the difference on a call. An SDR Agent works every interested reply, asks the questions you would ask, checks the account against the profile we agreed in writing, and books straight into your calendar. Where it is clearly not a fit, that conversation ends before it ever reaches you. Warm replies that want a phone conversation get followed up on the phone or on LinkedIn.

06
Yours

You keep the machine, not just the meetings

The domains, the inboxes, the lists, the campaigns and the written process all belong to you. You get a live dashboard showing meetings booked and where each one came from, so you are never taking our word for the numbers. If you walk away from us, the department stays behind.

The promise

We put the number in writing.

Before onboarding we agree how many qualified meetings we are committing to, and we define qualified so nobody argues about it later. It has to match the profile we signed off, be a real person at a real company, and actually turn up.

If we land under that number, you pick what happens. Either we keep the campaign running at no extra cost until we get there, or we refund the difference. Your call, not ours.

The number itself depends on your market, your deal size and how narrow the profile is, so we set it on the first call rather than publishing one figure and pretending it fits everybody.
