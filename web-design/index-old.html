Website design in Ireland: common questions.

How much does a website cost in Ireland?

You get a fixed quote before any work starts, based on the pages and features you need, so there are no surprises. Maintenance and hosting: a monthly fee, starting from the date your website goes live.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most sites go live within weeks, not months, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back. You see a live preview early and review it as often as you like.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours to keep. There's no lock-in.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes, without losing what already works. We rebuild on your existing domain and redirect old pages so Google keeps finding you.

Will my website show up on Google?

We make sure nothing on your site holds you back: every page is built to Google's standards and set up in Search Console and your Business Profile. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking, but you get a monthly report on how it's doing.

Do you build AI chat assistants as well?

Yes. We build AI chat assistants and voice agents that answer questions, capture leads and book appointments. They work on a new site or plug into the one you already have. Try the live demo on this page.
