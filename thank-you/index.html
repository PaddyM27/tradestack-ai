Before we talk

Straight answers to the questions people ask most.

Open the ones that matter to you. Anything we don't cover here, bring it to the call.

01 · Cost VideoWhat does it cost?How you pay, and why we do it this way.

Upfront, it's a one-off setup fee, then you pay per qualified appointment that actually happens. If they don't turn up, you don't pay for that appointment.

Your ad budget goes straight to Meta from your own card, so you see every cent. We never mark it up.

Why start this way? Most agencies get paid whether you get work or not. For the first 90 days, we only get paid when you're in front of a buyer, so you see it working before anything changes.

After the first 90 days, we'll talk about how we keep working together. We'll go through all of it on the call: what it would cost for your business and what it should bring back.

02 · Guarantee VideoHow does the guarantee actually work?What it covers, and what it doesn't.

Before you start, we research your area and forecast your best, average and lowest case. Your lowest forecast becomes your guaranteed minimum number of qualified appointments, in writing.

If we're short at day 90, we keep running everything with no extra management fee until we hit it.

What it doesn't cover: we guarantee appointments, not sales. Closing is down to you, and it applies when you follow up with and turn up to every appointment we book.

03 · Risk VideoWhat if it doesn't work for my business?The honest answer, and what happens next.

It doesn't work for everyone. When it fails, it's usually for one of three reasons: the jobs are too small for the numbers to add up, the business can't take on more work, or nobody's following up and closing.

That's why we do the forecast first. If the lowest case doesn't make you money, we'll tell you not to do it. If we take you on and fall behind, the guarantee kicks in.

And you own everything we build: ad account, CRM, automations and leads. If we ever part ways, you keep all of it.

04 · Who we are VideoWho are you, and are you legit?What you'll find when you look us up.

We're Paddy and Liam, based in Kingscourt, Co. Cavan. Paddy runs the Meta ads. Liam builds the systems and comes from years in sales. You deal with us directly, nothing is handed off to an account manager.

When you look us up, you'll find video reviews from clients we've built for, and our address and phone number on every page. Ring us any time before the call. See it all below.

05 · Your part VideoWhat do I need to do?Less than you'd think, but not nothing.

You fund the ads, and give us about an hour at the start plus photos, approvals and access. We aim to go live within 14 days of getting those.

After that, your job is to turn up to the appointments and close. We handle the ads, the replies, the calls and the booking.

06 · Contract VideoWhat am I signing up for?The commitment, and who owns what we build.

It starts with a 90-day commitment. That's how long it takes to build, launch and properly tune everything, and it's the window your guarantee covers.

Whatever we build is yours: your ad account, your CRM, your automations and your leads.

We'll go through the exact terms on the call, in writing, before you sign anything.
